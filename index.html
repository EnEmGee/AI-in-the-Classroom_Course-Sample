About AI in the Classroom Sample Course

This is a sample taster of AI in the Classroom, a short course coming in 2027. It gives you a taste of the ideas, features and learning experiences you will encounter in the full course.

Artificial intelligence (AI) is not a mere extension of existing tools but a shift that accelerates how information is produced, evaluated and interacted with, demanding new cognitive and educational responses. It can enhance learning design, support diverse learners, and reduce workload associated with repetitive or highly structured tasks. Yet AI also raises important classroom questions about accuracy, bias, privacy, wellbeing, appropriate dependence and academic integrity.

In the full 20-hour short course, you will explore practical ways to use AI purposefully across classroom practice while considering the professional judgement and responsibilities that remain with the teacher.

  • Experience how video, reflection and interactive digital activities are used to support learning in the full course.

Time commitment

This taster should take you around 10 minutes to complete. People learn at different rates, so take the time you need to explore the content and activities before moving on.
